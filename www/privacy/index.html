Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Crank & Catch collects nothing. The game makes no network connections, and nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

This policy covers the Crank & Catch game for Playdate and this web page, mrballistic.com/crank-and-catch. Crank & Catch is made by Todd Greco (mrballistic, “we,” “us” or “our”).

1. The game

Crank & Catch runs entirely on your Playdate. It contains no networking code, so it never connects to the internet. Specifically, it has:

  • No analytics, usage tracking or telemetry
  • No crash reporting
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No third-party services or advertising

The only thing it saves is your high score, which stays on your Playdate in the game’s own data. Deleting the game removes it.

2. This website

If you accept analytics cookies, this page uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Google processes that data under its own privacy policy. If you decline, Google’s code isn’t loaded at all and no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is stored in your browser so we don’t ask again, and you can change it at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page.

The same choice applies to the other pages on mrballistic.com, so if you’ve already answered there, you won’t be asked again here.

Nothing from the game is ever sent to the website.

3. Downloads and installing

The game is downloaded from GitHub, and also available on itch.io. Installing it on a Playdate goes through your account at play.date, run by Panic. Those services have their own privacy policies; we don’t receive any personal details from them. Downloading doesn’t require an account or an email address from us.

4. Children’s privacy

Because the game collects nothing, it doesn’t knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under the age required where you live). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we’ll delete it.

5. Your rights

Since the game doesn’t collect or store any personal data, there’s nothing for you to access, correct or delete. To remove website analytics, decline in “Cookie settings” and clear this site’s cookies in your browser.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
